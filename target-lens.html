Why the boundary is exact: each error is the distance from target to its estimate. Between two different estimates, those distances match at their midpoint: (mean + median) ÷ 2 = 53.3. On either side, the nearer estimate has smaller error. When mean and median are equal, every target ties.

Constructive proof / same five readings

Three targets. Three exact verdicts.

Place the target at either estimate or their midpoint to prove the possible outcomes.

Why these witnesses work for every fixed set of five readings:

|mean − target|² − |median − target|² = (mean − median)(mean + median − 2×target)

This proves a statement about the chosen readings and hypothetical targets. It does not verify a target or compare estimators on observed populations.

|mean − target|² − |median − target|² = (mean − median)(mean + median − 2×target)

The sum and median are monotone in every reading, giving exact bounds for the second factor. The first factor is optimized over all 30 choices of a median and two lower readings; within each ordering, its extrema occur at the limiting median values. The subset proof runs this exact certificate for all 32 choices of movable readings, freezing the others. If a factor crosses zero, the displayed rational fraction locates a tie along a path between its extrema.
