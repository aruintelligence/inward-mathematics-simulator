02 / Established identities

Past the finite grid.

Two familiar identities admit general arguments over real numbers. An exact symbolic checker validates their recorded algebra and every index reordering. A separate Lean module proves the midpoint identity and its conditional error comparisons for all real estimates and targets. Reordering invariance has a symbolic check here, but is not formalized in Lean. These established results are separate from the finite search statuses below.

A midpoint always ties.

For any two real estimates a and b, compare squared absolute errors at target t:

(a−t)² − (b−t)² = (a−b)(a+b−2t)

At t = (a+b)/2 the second factor vanishes, so the absolute errors are equal.

Order cannot change these estimates.

Reordering five real readings preserves their sum and sorted multiset. The mean and middle sorted value are unchanged, and so are their absolute errors at every target.

mean = (r₁+r₂+r₃+r₄+r₅)/5
