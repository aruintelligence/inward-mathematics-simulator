03 / Reproduce

Take the evidence with you

The partition includes every median transition and exact factor root. Each open interval has a constant winner; the printed positions of the bars are approximate, while the exported fractions are exact. A nearest threshold is a tie and is an infimum of movement, usually not an attained smallest strict reversal.

Check a downloaded certificate independently, browse your local Research Archive, or run the Python checker from the repository root:

python3 tools/verify_odd_sample_flip.py odd-sample-flip-certificate.json

Saving rechecks the certificate with a separate browser verifier and keeps it in this device's local archive. A passing checker verifies the mathematical claim under the entered inputs. The download itself does not authenticate measurements or prove an empirical theory. Explore the checked even-sample report, the signed example, or read the proof argument and limits.
