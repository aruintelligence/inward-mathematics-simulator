Inward Mathematics™

Two inspectable experiments: a damped attractor responding to seeded shocks, and five observers comparing ordinary mean with median under an adjustable outlier.

These are toy calculations with known inputs. The displayed “coherence” is a distance transform; neither panel measures memory, consciousness, safety, or a physical law.
