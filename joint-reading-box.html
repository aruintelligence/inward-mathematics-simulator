What will the proof say?

The two estimators are recomputed across every real configuration inside your chosen ranges. If an exact tie can occur, you receive readings and a target that attain it. Otherwise the new receipt gives the strict winner, the exact smallest squared-error lead, and readings and target where that minimum occurs.

The calculation runs on this device. The target and ranges are assumptions you choose; the page does not know whether they represent real measurements. The target may vary independently of the readings.

What this establishes

A conditional result across infinitely many configurations

The exact finite calculation covers every real reading and target inside the supplied intervals. A strict v3 verdict includes the exact attained minimum squared-error lead; legacy v1/v2 receipts retain their conservative bound. A tie result includes attainable readings and target. The receipt does not authenticate measurements, assign probabilities, or establish which estimator is appropriate for a real problem.
