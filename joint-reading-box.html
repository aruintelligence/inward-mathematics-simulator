What will the proof say?

The two estimators are recomputed across every real configuration inside your chosen ranges. If an exact tie can occur, you receive readings and a target that attain it. Otherwise the receipt gives the strict winner, the exact smallest squared-error lead, and readings and target where that minimum occurs.

To map the whole target range, enter a strictly higher “Target through” value and choose “Map every target.” The map finds every tie target and each exact linear piece of the worst-case lead, with readings that attain it. Then choose a required lead to find the exact target regions that guarantee it.

The calculation runs on this device. The target and ranges are assumptions you choose; the page does not know whether they represent real measurements. The target may vary independently of the readings.

What this establishes

A conditional result across infinitely many configurations

The exact finite calculation covers every real reading and target inside the supplied intervals. A strict v3 verdict includes the exact attained minimum squared-error lead; legacy v1/v2 receipts retain their conservative bound. A v4 map can answer a required-lead query and export exact target regions linked to the checked source JSON. A tie result includes attainable readings and target. The receipt does not authenticate measurements, assign probabilities, or establish which estimator is appropriate for a real problem.
