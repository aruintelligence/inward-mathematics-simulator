How to read this: 128 consecutive seeds (wrapping modulo 2³²) each produce four pseudorandom values in [42, 58). Every seed is tested against every integer fifth reading from 0 through 100. The colors compare absolute error against the chosen target 50. These 12,928 cells are a deterministic grid, not independent real observations or probabilities. A lower error in one cell, or more winning cells, does not establish a generally superior estimator.
